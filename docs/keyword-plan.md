## 1. Keyword Strategy Overview

This keyword architecture aligns organic search intent across high-intent commercial and informational queries. Keywords are mapped directly to dedicated service routes without cannibalization or keyword stuffing.

### Placement Guidelines Followed Across Pages:
- **Title Tag**: Primary keyword placed within the first 50–60 characters, followed by brand suffix (`| NEXVIX Innovations`).
- **H1 Heading**: Exactly one H1 per page containing the core entity keyword.
- **First 100 Words**: Primary keyword introduced contextually in the opening paragraph.
- **H2 Subheading**: At least one H2 per page containing a secondary variation or long-tail keyword.
- **Meta Description**: Primary keyword included within 140–160 characters with a clear conversion hook.
